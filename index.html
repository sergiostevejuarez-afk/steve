02 / SCIENTIFIC APPROACH

From physical model to candidate selection

An original simplified explanation, deliberately omitting internal material data and manufacturing details.

STEP 01

Define design candidates

Represent winding-related and geometric factors through a bounded nine-parameter optimization vector.

STEP 02

Estimate transformer response

Combine a nonlinear magnetic description with a transformer equivalent circuit. No proprietary data tables are shown.

STEP 03

Assess standardized burdens

Compute ratio and phase-related performance for the no-load case and the W, M, X and Y rated burdens.

STEP 04

Perform evolutionary search

Use selection, crossover, mutation and elitism to search for lower aggregate computational error.

◇Privacy by design. This overview does not reproduce internal M4 magnetic data, optimized physical parameters, engineering drawings, CAD files, software source code, or the submitted/IEEE-formatted manuscript.
